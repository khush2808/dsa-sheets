Privacy Policy

Your privacy is respected by default.

DSA Sheets (available via web and mobile app) is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not track you, run advertisements, or collect your personal data.

Privacy Overview

1. Data Collection

We do not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal identity data (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or IP addresses).

2. Local On-Device Storage

Your problem progress (completed checkmarks, notes, and topic filters) is saved strictly on your own device using local storage (localStorage on web and AsyncStorage on mobile). This data never leaves your device and is not sent to external servers.

3. Third-Party Links & Services

The application provides direct links to external websites for educational resources (such as LeetCode, takeUforward, and YouTube). When you click these external links, their respective privacy policies apply. We do not pass any user data to these third parties.

4. Analytics & Ads

DSA Sheets contains zero third-party tracking scripts, zero advertising networks, and zero telemetry data collection.

5. Contact & Support

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to open an issue or reach out via our GitHub repository: github.com/khush2808/dsa-sheets.
